Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our drone services

How quickly can you start a project?

For standard projects, we can typically begin within 3-5 business days. Emergency and urgent projects can be accommodated within 24-48 hours, subject to weather conditions and regulatory approvals.

What areas do you serve?

We provide drone services across India, with primary operations in Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Punjab, and Delhi NCR. We can deploy teams to any location based on project requirements.

Do you handle regulatory approvals?

Yes, we manage all necessary DGCA permissions, NOCs, and regulatory compliance for your project. Our team ensures all flights are conducted within legal frameworks.

What deliverables do you provide?

Depending on the service, we provide high-resolution images, videos, 3D models, orthomosaics, point clouds, GIS data, detailed reports, and analysis. All data is delivered in industry-standard formats.
